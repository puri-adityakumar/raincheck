This is Raincheck. A small, open-source desk for cancelling a plan kindly, and offering one real next time.
You say who it's for, and what the plan was. Pick a city, and the forecast comes straight from Open-Meteo. Open data, signed by the server.
AI drafting is switched off in this build. The desk says so plainly, and you write the note yourself.
You read the live preview, tick two checks, and only then does it make one expiring link. Nothing is sent for you.
Your friend opens the link, and accepts with one click. No account. No calendar writes. Just a tentative calendar file.
Good plans, another day. Raincheck is open source on GitHub.
